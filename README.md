# Krishi – Sell Thoughtfully

> A voice-first, channel-neutral decision copilot that supports farmers from planning to selling

## 🌾 The Problem

Farmers face three critical decisions that determine their livelihood:

1. **What to grow?** (Planning) - Which crops will be profitable? How much investment needed?
2. **When to sell?** (Harvest) - Sell now or wait? What risks exist?
3. **Where to sell?** (Market Access) - Who's paying best? Which buyers are reliable?

**Prices are visible. Risk, trade-offs, and opportunities are not.**

As a result, farmers often:
- Sell early to avoid uncertainty
- Make fear-driven decisions
- Miss better opportunities
- Regret outcomes when it's too late

## 💡 The Solution

**Krishi is a voice-first agricultural decision copilot** that provides:

### 1. **Selling Copilot** (MVP - 4 weeks)
- Scenario-based reasoning (sell now vs wait)
- Risk assessment and loss-reduction actions
- Channel-neutral information
- Directional price signals
- Transparent uncertainty communication

### 2. **Market Intelligence** (Phase 2 - 3-6 months)
- Recent transaction data from nearby farmers
- Active demand signals from verified buyers
- Network visualization of all channels
- Supply chain transparency
- Collective selling opportunities

### 3. **Crop Planning** (Phase 3 - 6-12 months)
- Seasonal demand intelligence
- Investment calculator with cost breakdown
- ROI projections with risk assessment
- Cultivation requirements
- Value-addition opportunities
- Industry applications and buyers

## 🎯 Core Principles

1. **Explain outcomes, not instructions** - Present scenarios, not commands
2. **Present risks before prices** - Lead with what could go wrong
3. **Communicate uncertainty clearly** - Be honest about limitations
4. **Preserve farmer autonomy** - Farmer always makes final decision
5. **Maintain channel neutrality** - No bias toward any selling option
6. **Voice-first, regional language** - Natural conversation in Telugu

## 🗣️ Voice-First Design

### Why Voice?
- Natural for farmers working in fields
- Works on any phone (smartphone or feature phone)
- Handles noisy field environments
- 85%+ speech recognition accuracy target
- Hands-free operation

### Voice Capabilities
- **Phone IVR**: Call toll-free number from any phone
- **In-App Voice**: Voice input/output in Android app
- **Voice Search**: 🎤 mic button for crop exploration
- **Voice Feedback**: 🎤 mic button for post-harvest outcomes
- **SMS/WhatsApp**: Text fallback for feature phones

## 🚀 How It Works

### MVP: Selling Decision Flow

1. **Farmer shares details** (voice or text):
   ```
   Voice: "టమాటా ఐదు వందల కిలోలు ఉంది"
   (I have 500 kg of tomatoes)
   ```

2. **Krishi presents two scenarios**:
   - Sell now
   - Wait briefly (24h, 48h, or 72h)

3. **For each scenario, explains**:
   - Directional price signals (rising/falling/stable)
   - Risk assessment (spoilage, weather, transport)
   - Loss-reduction actions with costs
   - Channel options (mandis, aggregators, traders)
   - What is uncertain and why

4. **Farmer decides independently**
   - No commands, no pressure
   - SMS summary sent after interaction
   - Outcome tracked for learning
