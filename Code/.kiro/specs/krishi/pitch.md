# **Krishi**

### Sell thoughtfully. 

---

## **Problem**

Farmers face three critical decisions that determine their livelihood:

**1. What to grow?** (Planning)
* Which crops will be profitable this season?
* How much investment do I need?
* Is my land suitable for this crop?

**2. When to sell?** (Harvest)
* Sell now or wait a few days?
* What could go wrong if I delay?
* How do spoilage, weather, and transport affect my crop today?

**3. Where to sell?** (Market Access)
* Who's paying the best price?
* Which buyers are reliable?
* Are there better opportunities I don't know about?

Prices are visible. **Risk, trade-offs, and opportunities are not.**

As a result, farmers often:

* Plant crops without understanding investment or returns
* Sell early to avoid uncertainty
* Choose familiar buyers over better options
* Make fear-driven decisions
* Miss value-addition opportunities
* Regret outcomes when it's too late

No platform supports farmers **across the complete farming cycle** - from planning to selling.

---

## **Solution**

**Krishi is a voice-first, channel-neutral decision copilot that supports farmers from planning to selling.**

Krishi provides three core capabilities:

**1. Crop Planning (Explore)** - Before planting
* Seasonal demand intelligence
* Investment calculator with cost breakdown
* ROI projections with risk assessment
* Cultivation requirements and suitability check
* Value-addition opportunities
* Industry applications and buyers

**2. Selling Copilot** - At harvest
* Scenario-based reasoning (sell now vs wait)
* Risk assessment and loss-reduction actions
* Channel-neutral information
* Directional price signals
* Uncertainty communication

**3. Market Intelligence** - Ongoing
* Recent transaction data from nearby farmers
* Active demand signals from buyers
* Network visualization of all channels
* Supply chain transparency
* Collective selling opportunities

Krishi provides **reasoning, not recommendations**, so farmers stay in control.

## **Detailed Flows**

### **Flow 1: Selling Decision (MVP)**

1. **Farmer shares details** (voice or text):
   * Crop type, quantity, location
   * Harvest date, storage conditions
   * Voice: "టమాటా ఐదు వందల కిలోలు ఉంది"
   * Text: Form-based input

2. **Krishi presents two scenarios**:
   * Sell now
   * Wait briefly (24h, 48h, or 72h)

3. **For each scenario, Krishi explains**:
   * Directional price signals (rising, falling, stable, uncertain)
   * Risk assessment (spoilage, weather, transport)
   * Practical loss-reduction actions with costs (₹50-100, ₹100-200)
   * Channel options (mandis, aggregators, traders, direct buyers)
   * What is uncertain and why (data age, limitations)

4. **Farmer decides independently**
   * No commands, no pressure
   * SMS summary sent after interaction
   * Outcome tracked for learning (optional)
   * Voice feedback option for post-harvest outcomes (🎤 speak instead of type)

### **Flow 2: Market Intelligence (Phase 2)**

1. **Recent Transactions**:
   * See what nearby farmers sold (anonymized)
   * Crop type, quantity, price, channel, location, date
   * Average price calculation
   * Privacy-preserving (opt-in sharing)

2. **Demand Signals**:
   * Active buyer requirements
   * Buyer type, location, quantity needed
   * Price range, urgency, quality requirements
   * Direct contact information
   * Verified buyers only

3. **Network Visualization**:
   * Map view of all channels
   * List view with ratings and details
   * Supply chain pathways
   * Alternative routes comparison
   * Reliability scores (5-star ratings)

4. **Collective Selling**:
   * Join existing farmer groups
   * Create new demand-based groups
   * Match with bulk buyers
   * Better prices through volume
   * Transparent group management

### **Flow 3: Crop Planning (Phase 3)**

1. **Explore Home**:
   * Seasonal demand dashboard
   * Regional trends (what neighbors are growing)
   * Profitable crops (ROI > 200%)
   * Voice search with 🎤 mic button: "ఈ సీజన్‌లో ఏ పంటలకు డిమాండ్ ఎక్కువ?"
   * Hands-free crop exploration while working

2. **Crop Details**:
   * Demand level (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
   * Season and price expectations
   * Investment overview
   * ROI preview
   * Quick access to calculator

3. **Investment Calculator**:
   * Land size input (acres)
   * Resource availability (irrigation)
   * Cost breakdown by category:
     - Seeds/seedlings
     - Fertilizers
     - Pesticides
     - Irrigation
     - Labor
     - Equipment
     - Miscellaneous
   * Total investment range (min-max)
   * Voice: "టమాటా ఒక ఎకరా పెట్టుబడి ఎంత?"

4. **ROI Projections**:
   * Expected yield range
   * Expected price range
   * Total revenue calculation
   * Net profit after costs
   * ROI percentage
   * Risk assessment (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
   * Break-even analysis
   * Voice: "మిరపకాయ లాభం ఎంత?"

5. **Cultivation Requirements**:
   * Soil type, pH, drainage
   * Water needs, irrigation frequency
   * Temperature, rainfall, sunlight
   * Season suitability
   * Land suitability check
   * Voice: "నా భూమికి ఏ పంట సరిపోతుంది?"

6. **Value Addition**:
   * Dried/processed forms
   * Price multipliers (2x, 3x, 5x)
   * Equipment needed
   * Processing costs
   * Market access
   * Success stories

7. **Industry Applications**:
   * Food processing companies
   * Hotels and restaurants
   * Export opportunities
   * Quality requirements
   * Price premiums
   * Direct buyer contacts

No dashboards. No commands. Just clarity in the farmer's language.

## **Why This Is Different**

**Voice-First for Rural Farmers**
* Natural conversation in regional language
* Works on any phone (smartphone or feature phone)
* Handles noisy field environments
* 85%+ speech recognition accuracy target
* Voice search on Explore page for hands-free crop planning
* Voice feedback on outcome screen for spoken comments
* Seamless voice-to-text with edit capability

**Channel-Neutral**
* No buying, selling, or commissions
* Information about ALL selling options
* No bias toward any channel
* Builds trust through neutrality

**Honest About Uncertainty**
* Explicitly states what the system doesn't know
* Shows data age ("market data from 2 days ago")
* Uses phrases like "we don't know" and "this is uncertain"
* Never overstates certainty

**Offline-Capable**
* Works with cached data when internet is unavailable
* Syncs when connectivity is restored
* Essential for rural areas with unreliable networks

**Risk-First, Not Price-First**
* Leads with what could go wrong
* Explains spoilage, weather, and transport risks
* Provides practical loss-reduction actions
* Helps farmers protect income, not chase prices

**Designed for Messy Rural Data**
* Works with incomplete or outdated information
* Uses neighboring region data when local data unavailable
* Provides qualified guidance rather than refusing to help
* Prioritizes honesty over false precision

Trust is the product.

## **Technology Stack**

**Voice Services (AWS)**
* Amazon Connect: IVR system, toll-free number
* Amazon Transcribe: Speech-to-text (regional language)
* Amazon Polly: Text-to-speech (natural voice)
* Amazon Lex: Dialogue management

**Backend (AWS Serverless)**
* Lambda: Python 3.11, serverless compute
* API Gateway: REST APIs
* Cognito: Phone OTP authentication
* WAF: Basic security

**Storage (AWS)**
* RDS PostgreSQL: Farmer profiles
* ElastiCache Redis: Caching
* DynamoDB: Market data, outcomes
* S3: Historical data, call recordings

**Mobile**
* Android: Kotlin/Java, offline-first (SQLite)
* Voice input/output capabilities
* Regional language support

**External APIs**
* e-NAM: Mandi prices
* OpenWeather: Weather forecasts

**Cost-Efficient**
* ₹25,000-45,000/month (~$300-550)
* Serverless architecture
* Simple rule-based reasoning (no expensive ML)
* Aggressive caching

## **Data & Defensibility**

Krishi is built for imperfect data:

* Directional price & arrival trends (not predictions)
* Weather risk signals (thresholds)
* Crop-specific spoilage behavior (rules)

Over time, Krishi learns from:

* What scenarios were presented
* What farmers chose
* What actually happened

This creates a **crop- and region-specific decision intelligence layer** that:
* Compounds with each harvest cycle
* Becomes more accurate over time
* Is hard to replicate
* Preserves farmer privacy (anonymized data)

**Competitive Moats**
