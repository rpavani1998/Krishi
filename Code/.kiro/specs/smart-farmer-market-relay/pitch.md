# Smart Farmer–Market Relay: AI-Powered Selling Copilot

## The Problem We're Solving

**Farmers lose 30-40% of their income due to poor selling decisions.**

Every harvest, farmers face the same critical questions:
- **When should I sell?** (Wait for better prices or sell before spoilage?)
- **Where should I sell?** (Local market, aggregator, or direct buyer?)
- **What price should I expect?** (Am I getting a fair deal?)

Without data-driven insights, farmers often:
- Sell too early at low prices due to fear of spoilage
- Miss peak demand periods
- Get exploited by middlemen
- Lack access to better-paying buyers

## Our Solution: An AI Selling Copilot for Farmers

**Smart Farmer–Market Relay is like having a market expert in your pocket.**

### What It Does
- **Analyzes** your harvest details, market conditions, and weather patterns
- **Recommends** the optimal time, place, and price to sell
- **Explains** why in simple language you can trust
- **Connects** you to the best buyers (markets, aggregators, direct buyers)
- **Works offline** so rural connectivity isn't a barrier

### How It Works (Simple 3-Step Process)

1. **📱 Input Your Harvest**
   - Crop type, quantity, quality, location
   - Takes 2 minutes on your phone

2. **🤖 AI Analyzes Everything**
   - Current market prices across all channels
   - Weather impact on prices and spoilage
   - Transportation costs and buyer demand
   - Historical patterns and seasonal trends

3. **💡 Get Clear Recommendations**
   - "Sell tomatoes in 3-5 days at Kolar mandi for ₹25-30/kg"
   - "Confidence: 85% - High demand due to festival season"
   - "Alternative: Wait 1 week for aggregator at ₹32/kg (70% confidence)"
