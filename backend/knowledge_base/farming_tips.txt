# Tomato Harvest Guidelines

## When to Harvest
- Harvest tomatoes when they reach the "Breaker" stage (pink color starting to show) if transporting long distances.
- For local markets, harvest at "Red Ripe" stage for best flavor.
- Avoid harvesting immediately after rain to prevent fungal infections.

## Storage
- Store ripe tomatoes at 12-15°C.
- Do not refrigerate below 10°C as it causes chilling injury.
- Shelf life is 1-2 weeks depending on variety.

## Common Diseases
- Early Blight: Dark spots on leaves. Use Copper Oxychloride.
- Fruit Borer: Holes in fruit. Use pheromone traps.

# Onion Market Trends (Andhra Pradesh)

## Madanapalle Market
- Major hub for Tomato and Onion.
- Peak season: August-October.
- Current average price for onions: ₹2500/quintal.

## Post-Harvest Handling
- Curing: Dry onions in the field for 3-5 days to remove excess moisture.
- Removes field heat and tightens the neck.
- Reduces storage losses by 20%.
