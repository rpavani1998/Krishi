# Authentic Agricultural Knowledge Sources

## 1. Government of India (Official Data)
- **eNAM (National Agriculture Market)**: [https://enam.gov.in](https://enam.gov.in)
  - Real-time mandi arrivals, modal prices, and trade volumes.
  - *Best for*: Current market prices and trends.
- **Agmarknet**: [https://agmarknet.gov.in](https://agmarknet.gov.in)
  - Daily price reports from APMCs across India.
  - *Best for*: Historical price analysis.
- **IMD (India Meteorological Department)**: [https://mausam.imd.gov.in](https://mausam.imd.gov.in)
  - District-level weather forecasts (Meghdoot App).
  - *Best for*: Rainfall alerts, temperature, and humidity forecasts.
- **ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)**: [https://icar.org.in](https://icar.org.in)
  - Crop-specific advisories, pest management, and new varieties.
  - *Best for*: Scientific farming practices.

## 2. Andhra Pradesh Specific Sources
- **ANGRAU (Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University)**: [https://angrau.ac.in](https://angrau.ac.in)
  - Localized crop calendars and pest alerts for AP districts.
  - *Best for*: Region-specific advice (e.g., Rayalaseema vs Coastal Andhra).
- **Dr. YSR Rythu Bharosa Kendra (RBK)**:
  - Village-level input shops and advisory centers.
  - *Best for*: Input availability and government schemes.

## 3. International & Research Bodies
- **FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization)**: [https://www.fao.org](https://www.fao.org)
  - Global food price index and supply chain reports.
  - *Best for*: Understanding global export/import trends.
- **CGIAR (Consultative Group on International Agricultural Research)**:
  - Advanced research on climate-resilient crops.

## 4. Market Intelligence & News
- **The Hindu Business Line (Agri-Business)**: [https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/agri-business/](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/agri-business/)
  - Policy changes (e.g., Onion export bans), trade analysis.
- **Financial Express (Commodities)**:
  - Futures market trends (NCDEX/MCX).
- **Reuters (Global Commodities)**:
  - Global supply shocks (e.g., El Niño impact on tomato production).
