# Market Intelligence & News (March 2026)

## Policy & Trade Alerts
- **Onion Export Policy**:
  - *Status (2026-02-15)*: Government of India has extended the **ban on onion exports** indefinitely due to low Rabi crop estimates and rising domestic prices.
  - *Impact*: Prices expected to stabilize in domestic markets (₹20-30/kg) but crash in major producing hubs (Nashik/Kurnool) due to glut.
  - *Recommendation*: Farmers in AP advised to store quality onions for 2-3 months using improved storage structures if possible.

- **Tomato Minimum Export Price (MEP)**:
  - *Status (2026-01-20)*: No MEP currently. Exports to Bangladesh/Nepal allowed freely.
  - *Market*: High demand from processing industries in Chittoor district due to global shortage of tomato paste.
  - *Price Trend*: Upward trend expected until April harvest arrivals.

## Weather & Pest Warnings
- **El Niño Alert (IMD)**:
  - *Forecast*: Mild El Niño conditions likely to persist until June 2026.
  - *Risk*: Possibility of delayed monsoon onset in Rayalaseema region.
  - *Crop Impact*: Groundnut and Cotton sowing may be delayed. Tomato yields could be lower due to heat stress in flowering stage.

- **Pest Outbreak (Telangana/AP Border)**:
  - *Alert*: Severe incidence of **Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda)** reported in Maize crops near Kurnool.
  - *Cross-Crop Risk*: Can migrate to nearby Tomato/Chilli fields if not controlled.
  - *Advisory*: Monitor fields twice weekly. Use Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4g/L if ETL crossed.

## Global Supply Chain
- **Red Sea Crisis**:
  - *Impact*: Shipping delays for agri-exports to Europe/Middle East. Freight costs up by 15%.
  - *Commodities Affected*: Grapes, Pomegranates, and processed Mango pulp.
  - *Local Effect*: Exporters reducing procurement prices to offset shipping costs.
